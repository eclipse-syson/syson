On ratio scales for one kind of quantity that only differ in their unit (e.g. metre and inch) &#xA;  zero is zero no matter what unit is selected.&#xA;- A unit may carry a conversion factor definition w.r.t. to another reference unit. It can be a conversion by convention (e.g. between metre and foot) or &#xA;  via an ISO/IEC 80000 prefix symbol that indicates a decimal or binary multiple or sub-multiple (e.g. kilo, nano, mega, kibi, mebi, ...).
